Spanish law (Royal Decree 513/2017) requires residential communities, hotels, and commercial establishments to maintain a valid maintenance agreement with a registered fire safety contractor. Without this certification, insurance companies can void policies in the event of fire. We deliver clear technical reports in English for international community presidents and property managers.
